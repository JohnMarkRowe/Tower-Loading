Radio Communications Tower Loading Study

Wind & ice load estimation across wind speed and ice thickness increments

Preliminary planning tool — not a substitute for a licensed Professional Engineer. This tool estimates wind and ice load demand using standard TIA‑222 / ASCE 7 methodology. It does not contain manufacturer tower capacity ratings — you must supply your tower's allowable moment/weight capacity from its original engineering data, a manufacturer load chart, or a PE evaluation. Tower foundations, guy anchors, and other supporting structures are assumed adequate. Do not add, remove, or modify tower loading based solely on the output of this tool.
